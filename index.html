About Texas Promise Watch

We track the campaign promises made by the winners of Texas's 2026 elections and check every week whether each one is kept, broken, stalled or still in progress, with a link to the source for every update.

Every day we also explain one Texas political story in plain language: what's happening, who decides, and what it means for your money, schools and daily life.

Texas Promise Watch is independent. We are not affiliated with any party, campaign or government office.
