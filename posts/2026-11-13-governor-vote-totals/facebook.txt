Abbott held steady. Democrats added 1.7 million votes.

Greg Abbott's vote total for governor barely moved across the last three elections. The Democratic total jumped by about 1.7 million from 2022 to 2026.

Votes for Texas governor:
• 2018: Abbott (R) 4,656,196. Valdez (D) 3,546,615.
• 2022: Abbott (R) 4,437,099. O'Rourke (D) 3,553,656.
• 2026 (unofficial AP count): Hinojosa (D) 5,230,929 (52.8%). Abbott (R) 4,524,955 (45.7%).

From 2022 to 2026, Abbott gained about 88,000 votes (+2%). The Democratic nominee gained about 1.68 million (+47%). All votes cast for governor rose from 8.10 million to about 9.90 million.

These 2026 numbers are unofficial. In Texas, the governor's race becomes official only after the Legislature canvasses the returns, and it meets Jan. 12, 2027. Counts can still shift a little before then.

This chart shows vote totals only. It doesn't tell us why they changed.
