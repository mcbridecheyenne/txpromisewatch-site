Texas Gov.-elect Gina Hinojosa promised to freeze residential electricity rates on Day One. She told the Texas Tribune she'd declare a "high-cost emergency" within an hour of taking office Jan. 19, then have the state freeze home rates until data centers and other big users pay their own way. Texas homes paid 15.91 cents per kWh in August vs. 18.16 cents nationally (U.S. EIA). Supporters say families need relief now. Critics say price caps in a competitive market could discourage new power plants and end up in court. Promise status: not started. Sources: Texas Tribune via Fox News and Energy Choice Matters, U.S. EIA.
