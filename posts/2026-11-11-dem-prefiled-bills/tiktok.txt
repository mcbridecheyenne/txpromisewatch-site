Filed isn't passed. Texas Democrats pre-filed bills for the 2027 session to expand Medicaid, raise the minimum wage to $15 an hour, legalize and tax marijuana, and raise the minimum age to buy certain semi-automatic rifles to 21. These are proposals, not laws. Each one has to pass the Texas House and the Republican-held Senate and get past the governor. Republicans filed their own bills too, including property tax cuts. Sources: Texas Tribune and Marijuana Moment.
