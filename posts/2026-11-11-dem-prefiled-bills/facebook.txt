Filed isn't passed: what Texas Democrats proposed this week

Bill filing for the 2027 Texas legislative session opened Nov. 9, and Democrats pre-filed bills on several big issues:

• Expand Medicaid (House bill by Rep. John Bucy, Senate bill by Sen. Judith Zaffirini)
• Raise the state minimum wage to $15 an hour (HB 110, HB 132)
• Legalize, tax and regulate marijuana (HB 362, HB 369)
• Raise the minimum age to buy certain semi-automatic rifles to 21 (HB 173)

These are proposals, not laws. A bill has to pass the Texas House and Senate and get past the governor. Democrats now hold the House; Republicans still hold the Senate, where the Texas Tribune expects many of these ideas to face heavy resistance. Republicans filed their own bills too, including ones to cut or end property taxes.

You may have seen "20 gun bills." So far we could confirm the rifle-age bill only.
