Filed isn't passed: what Texas Democrats proposed this week

Bill filing for the 2027 Texas legislative session opened Nov. 9. Democrats pre-filed bills to:

• Expand Medicaid
• Raise the state minimum wage to $15 an hour (HB 110, HB 132)
• Legalize, tax and regulate marijuana (HB 362, HB 369)
• Raise the minimum age to buy certain semi-automatic rifles to 21 (HB 173)

None of these is law. A bill has to pass the Texas House and Senate and get past the governor. Democrats now hold the House; Republicans still hold the Senate, where many of these ideas are expected to face heavy resistance. Republicans filed their own bills too, including ones to cut or end property taxes.

The "20 gun bills" number going around? So far we could confirm the rifle-age bill only.
